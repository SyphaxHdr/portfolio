I was a level designer: I built the islands and the 3D search phases, a first for me. Production was run by another student, with Jira for most of the year; it had trouble keeping up with the 3D work, so I took over that follow-up. Being more at ease with sound design than the rest of the team, I also worked as the link with the sound designers and integrated the sound into the game.

The project went well, and it is where I noted the team problems I solved the next year on Amachahou: branches named without a real rule, files dropped in without anyone being told, large files pushed again every time a branch was picked up.
