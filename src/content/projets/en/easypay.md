A student start-up of 6: 4 developers and 2 graphic designers. I coordinated the team, designed the whole architecture, the UML diagrams and the database, then developed part of the interface.

The app was tested by the team, our teacher and the jury, and the project earned me the "Start-up" diploma from the University of Béjaïa. Online payment was still missing: we wanted to use Algérie Poste's Edahabia card, and we were in touch with them about a partnership. The start-up was never launched: five of the six team members, including me, left to study in France.
